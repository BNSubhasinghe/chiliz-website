Each member leads one component of the rover system and contributes to integration, field testing and the chiliz dashboard.

Arachci B. N. S.

IT22112682, Information Technology specialisation
Intelligent Navigation and Sensor-Based Irrigation Control
  • Built the GNSS reliability scoring and threshold-based drip irrigation logic.
  • Rebuilt the soil moisture anomaly pipeline on 3,346 records with 35 features.
  • Prepared Isolation Forest and Random Forest models with a live demo GUI.
it22112682@my.sliit.lk

Kulasekara D. K. M.

IT22108722, Information Technology specialisation
AI-Based Chilli Crop Disease, Stress and Growth Monitoring
  • Developed an EfficientNet-B0 model for an 8-class chilli disease dataset.
  • Implemented pre-processing, augmentation and class-wise evaluation.
  • Converted the model to TFLite with an interactive prediction demo.
it22108722@my.sliit.lk

S. G. M. B. Subasinghe

IT22276032, Information Technology specialisation
Field Zone Mapping and Autonomous Seeding and Treatment Planning
  • Processed 120,130 Sentinel-2 records using six spectral features.
  • Selected zones with elbow and silhouette analysis, reaching a score of 0.5423.
  • Designed the complete system flow and the interactive field-zone map.
it22276032@my.sliit.lk

S. N. R. Jayawardhana

IT22192950, Information Technology specialisation
Soil Health Prediction Using a Hybrid LSTM and XGBoost Model
  • Cleaned 20,000 NPK sensor records into 17,356 usable records.
  • Classified soil as healthy, moderate or poor from seven variables.
  • Reached 89.46% test accuracy with the LSTM + XGBoost hybrid model.
it22192950@my.sliit.lk
